Glossary

To guide those who wish to learn more...



Mughal Empire

 A major Muslim power that ruled over a large part of the Indian subcontinent from the 16th to the 19th century, responsible for building the Taj Mahal under Emperor Shah Jahan.

Marble

 A noble and precious stone used in the construction of the Taj Mahal, particularly the white marble from Rajasthan, famous for its purity and translucency.

Calligraphy

 The art of beautiful and aesthetic writing. The Taj Mahal is adorned with Quranic inscriptions crafted from black marble, symbolizing the entrance into a sacred space.

Minarets

 Slender towers traditionally associated with mosques. The four minarets of the Taj Mahal frame the mausoleum, serving both a decorative purpose and engineered to tilt slightly outward to prevent hitting the tomb in the event of an earthquake.

Cenotaph

 An empty symbolic tomb, often placed above the actual burial vault. The graves of Mumtaz Mahal and Shah Jahan inside the Taj Mahal are surmounted by richly decorated cenotaphs.

Multani Mitti

 A natural clay also known as "Fuller's earth", traditionally used in the Multan region (modern Pakistan) for its purifying and cosmetic properties, and occasionally for finishings in Mughal architecture.

Pietra Dura

 An architectural inlay technique using highly polished semi-precious stones to create complex, elegant, and durable floral geometric patterns in the white marble.

Qubba

 A dome, often central, symbolizing spiritual elevation. The main dome of the Taj Mahal is one of the most famous in the world for its harmonious shape.

Mihrab

 A wall niche indicating the direction of Mecca. In the mosque adjacent to the Taj Mahal, there is a mihrab oriented for prayer.

Charbagh

 A Mughal garden divided into four sections based on a symbolic representation of paradise, with the landscape arranged around the Taj Mahal.

Chhatri

 Small dome-shaped pavilions typical of Indo-Islamic architecture. Several can be found on the roof of the Taj Mahal, contributing to its elegant silhouette.

Iwan

 A large vaulted monumental arch framing an entryway, a central element in Islamic architecture. The Taj Mahal features several iwans on its facades.
