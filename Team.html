The Goal of This Project

This website was created as part of a university project conducted in partnership with UNESCO. It is designed as a professional role-playing exercise aimed at developing our skills in project management, digital communication, accessibility, and cultural mediation. Our goal is to provide a clear, accessible, and enriching platform for all audiences — travelers, the curious, researchers, or simply history enthusiasts — wishing to discover the Taj Mahal from a different perspective, whether before, during, or after their visit.
